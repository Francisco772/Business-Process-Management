# Home Burger: Order-to-Cash Process Optimization & Simulation

## Executive Summary
This project delivers a deep-dive structural analysis and strategic redesign of the **Order-to-Cash (OtC)** workflow for "Home Burger" a food delivery enterprise. By applying Business Process Management (BPM) principles, we identified critical operational bottlenecks in a manual system and engineered an automated "To-Be" model focused on resource optimization and cycle-time reduction.

---

## The Problem: "As-Is" Process Analysis
The initial discovery phase revealed a decentralized workflow where manual interventions compromised both scalability and accuracy:
* **Manual Handoffs:** Communication between customers, the kitchen, and delivery riders relied on manual verification, leading to high error rates and information silos.
* **Peak-Load Bottlenecks:** Quantitative simulations identified that during high-demand periods, the manual order-verification stage became a single point of failure.
* **Operational Blind Spots:** A lack of real-time tracking meant delivery windows were estimated rather than data-driven, impacting overall service reliability.

---

## Methodology: The BPM Life Cycle
We managed the transition from manual to automated using a structured **Waterfall project management** approach.

### 1. Process Discovery & BPMN Modeling
Using **BPMN 2.0 standards**, we mapped the end-to-end journey of an order:
* **As-Is Model:** Visualized the current state to pinpoint non-value-added tasks.
* **To-Be Model:** Designed a streamlined architecture introducing automated order validation and centralized dispatch logic.

### 2. Quantitative Simulation & Validation
To move beyond theoretical improvements, we conducted simulations to stress-test the redesigned model:
* **Throughput Analysis:** Modeled how the system handles significant volume increases compared to the manual baseline.
* **Lead-Time Reduction:** Quantified the decrease in total cycle time from order placement to final delivery.
* **Resource Optimization:** Demonstrated how reallocating staff from manual verification to production increases kitchen capacity.

### 3. Governance & Risk Management
A robust technical model requires a clear implementation roadmap:
* **Gantt Charts:** Established a timeline for software integration, staff training, and pilot testing.
* **RACI Matrix:** Defined clear accountability, ensuring alignment between technical and operational stakeholders.
* **Risk Assessment Matrix:** Identified potential failure points—such as system downtime or rider shortages—and established proactive mitigation strategies.
